When a loved one’s care needs begin to change, families may feel unsure how to step in while still respecting their wishes, routines, and sense of control. The right support at home can help individuals stay involved in the choices that shape their daily lives, whether they need help with everyday activities, skilled care after a health change, or comfort-focused support during a serious illness.
